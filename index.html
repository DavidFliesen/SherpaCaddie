Sherpa Caddie guides you through club choice, safe targets, and one clear mental cue — one shot at a time.

What makes Sherpa Caddie different?

  • A guide and mentor, not just a scorecard or GPS app.
  • Built for new golfers and golfers who want simpler, smarter decisions.
  • Teaches club selection, course reading, and mental reset while you play.
