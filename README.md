## Overview

Sherpa Caddie is an AI-inspired golf companion designed to guide golfers one shot at a time.

Instead of overwhelming players with statistics and complexity, Sherpa Caddie combines the guidance of a **Sherpa mentor** with the practical help of a **caddie**. It helps golfers choose a club, pick a smart target, calm their mind, and commit to the shot in front of them.

Whether it is your first round or your hundredth, Sherpa Caddie is built to make golf easier to understand and easier to enjoy.

---

## What Version 0.3 focuses on

Version 0.3 concentrates entirely on the **One-Shot Sherpa** system:

- Personal club-distance setup
- Simplified in-round screen
- Shot-quality buttons
- Club and safe-target recommendation
- One mental cue
- New-shot reset message
- Automatic scoring from recorded shots
- Three-item post-round summary

That establishes the feature that makes Sherpa Caddie genuinely different before adding more course maps, analytics, or AI complexity.

## Post-round summary language included

- **What cost strokes:** Most poor shots happened when you selected too little club.
- **Next-round focus:** Pick the target, take one practice swing, and commit.

---

## Philosophy

Sherpa Caddie is meant to be a trusted guide on the course.

It helps golfers:

- slow down
- choose a club with confidence
- read the safest part of the course
- learn from the shot they just hit
- reset for the next shot
- keep their minds from racing ahead

The app should teach as it guides.
